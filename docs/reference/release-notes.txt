0.9.1 — the design language: one type scale, four radii, one spacing grid.

Check the version first: long-press the app icon, App info. It must read 0.9.1.

Nothing new was added. Everything was made to agree with everything else.

The glass is a measured port now and it behaves like the source, so what was
left reading cheap was everything around it. It was measured, all 39 code
files. The fault was not any one value. It was that nothing in the app agreed
with anything else in it: 42 spacing values, 12 corner radii, and three text
sizes half a point apart. No single one of those is visible. The absence of
a system is.

The full measurement is docs/findings/2026-08-13-design-language.md.

Text now sits on one ratio, and the title outranks body text.

The brand in the top bar and your name on Settings were 14.5sp while reading
text was 15sp — set SMALLER than body. A screen cannot show a hierarchy the
scale does not have. Title is now 18sp.

Below it, four sizes crowded 12.5 to 15sp at ratios of 1.03 to 1.12. Half a
point is less than a reader can see, so four ranks read as one rank done
carelessly. Now: caption 10.5, label 12.5, body 15, title 18, heading 26 —
each step 1.2 times the last, which is the step size a reader can actually
see. The big numbers on the ticket and the Opened screen do not change: 122sp
is sized to be read across a corridor, not to fit a progression.

Corner radii went from twelve values to four: 10, 16, 22, 28, plus the pill.

Three of the old five were 13, 15 and 17 — two points apart, which nobody
holding a phone can tell apart. They bought inconsistency and paid nothing.
Cards are rounder now. The cabinet render sits in a deeper 28dp well.

Spacing went from 42 values to seven: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24.

69 of the 105 gaps in the app were off any grid — padding of 9, 10 and 11 in
neighbouring components, so every edge missed its neighbour by a pixel or
two. Never enough to notice. Always enough to stop it looking cut from one
piece.

The quiet grey text passed an accessibility standard it was failing.

That grey carries the nav bar's unselected labels — CABINET, SETTINGS — and
the small chips. It measured 2.63:1 on the light ground where the standard
asks 4.5:1, and 2.98:1 on a dark card. It now measures 4.54:1 and 4.50:1.
It is a real standards failure fixed, not a preference: that text was too
faint to read in daylight, which is where this app gets used.

Known, unchanged: the Dark mode switch on Settings is a speckled blob in dark
mode only. BUG-003.

New, and please check this one on your phone: on the emulator the cabinet
render on the sign-in screen fills only the left third of its box. It is
NOT from this change — this change moved that screen's margin by 1dp, which
cannot move a render by 200. It is most likely the emulator's software
renderer failing on a large image. If it looks right on your phone, say so
and it gets closed. BUG-004.
