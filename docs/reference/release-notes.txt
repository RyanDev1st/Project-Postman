0.10.0 — the app speaks Vietnamese, the map hands off, one door at a time.

Check the version first: long-press the app icon, App info. It must read 0.10.0.

THE APP IS IN VIETNAMESE NOW, and you can switch it while you watch.

Settings, App, Ngon ngu / Language. Pick one and every word changes on the
next frame with the sheet still under your thumb. No restart, and you do not
lose the screen you were on.

Both languages are written out in full: 85 strings in English, 82 in
Vietnamese, and the three that are missing are missing on purpose because
they are names — VGU Smart Locker, VGU Locker, Google. Every screen was
converted, not only Settings.

Each language is listed in itself, "English" and "Tieng Viet", never
"Vietnamese". Somebody hunting for their own language is looking for the word
they would write, and if they could read the other one they would not need
the row.

It follows the phone on first run. A phone set to Vietnamese opens the app in
Vietnamese without anyone choosing. Like Dark mode, the choice lasts as long
as the app is running and is forgotten when it is closed — neither setting is
written down yet, and they want one place to be written down together.

The map's arrow works, and the walk figures are real.

The arrow on the map card looked like a button and did nothing: the whole
card, arrow included, just opened the map. Tapping it now hands off to
whichever maps app the phone has, with the gate as the destination. If there
is no maps app it says so rather than appearing to be broken.

The card used to say "7 min walk / 540 m". Nothing measured that. The real
route drawn underneath is 543 m and 6 min 32 s, so the caption was three
metres and half a minute away from the line on the picture. The distance is
now measured off that line — it comes out at 542.5 m against the router's own
543, which is also the check that the two agree. Moving a coordinate can no
longer leave the caption describing the old path.

It is still not Google's map, and that is on purpose — ADR 0014. Google
wanted a card on file for a map it charges nothing for.

On the cabinet tab, choosing a box puts the others out.

Tap 04 and the camera pushes in on it, as before, but 07 now fades out as it
goes — and comes back if you pull out to the wide shot. Two lit doors answers
"which of these are mine". Once you have chosen, the question is "which box
does the button open", and a second lit door was still answering the old one.
The light is tied to the camera itself, so the two are one movement.

Known, unchanged: the Dark mode switch on Settings is a speckled blob in dark
mode only. BUG-003.

Still worth a look on your phone: on the emulator the cabinet render on the
sign-in screen fills only the left third of its box. It is not from the
design pass — that moved the margin by 1dp, which cannot move a render by
200 — and it is most likely the emulator's software renderer failing on a
large image. If it looks right on your phone, say so and it closes. BUG-004.
