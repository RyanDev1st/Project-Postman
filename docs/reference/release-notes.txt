0.10.1 — the outline around every card was the wrong colour

This build is small and it is mostly one fix, but please look at it in dark
mode.

WHAT CHANGED

The thin outline drawn around every card, every input box, every number chip
and every piece of glass in the app was a teal, where the design says a pale
blue. One digit was typed wrong when the colours were first put in, and it has
been in every build you have seen. In dark mode it is on almost every edge on
screen. The shadow under a card in light mode was also slightly washed out,
and is now the design's own.

It was found by a check that compares all 46 of the app's colours against the
designer's original file by number rather than by eye. Six builds of looking
at screens never caught it. 45 of the 46 now match exactly; the one that does
not is a caption grey, made lighter on purpose because the original failed the
readability standard.

One gap in the map card that was not on the spacing grid is now on it.

WHAT WE NEED YOUR EYES ON

Four things can only be judged on a real phone, because they need the graphics
chip that a computer test does not have:

1. The bottom bar and the two round buttons in the top bar. They should look
   like frosted glass with the screen blurred behind them, not like dark
   discs. On the test computer they come out as dark discs, which is expected
   and tells us nothing.
2. The dark/light switch in Settings (BUG-003). It drew perfectly clean on the
   test computer, so the speckling you saw is probably the emulator and not
   the app. One look settles it.
3. The switch's halo circles (BUG-002), which should now be clipped inside the
   pill and not spread behind it.
4. The cabinet picture on the sign-in screen (BUG-004), which on the emulator
   fills only the left third of its box. We think that is the emulator too.

The cabinet screen itself is worth a look while you are there: tap a door and
the camera should push in on it while the other doors fade back.

ONE DECISION FOR YOU

The design pass put the app on one spacing grid and one set of corner radii.
Four values in the mock-up do not sit on either — the card's corner is 18 and
its padding 14, and the bottom bar's padding is 5 with a 3 gap. The app now
uses 22, 12, 4 and 2. Everything else matches the mock-up exactly.

So "match the mock-up" and "sit on a grid" now disagree, in four places, none
by more than 4dp. It has been written down and settled in favour of the grid,
because the mock-up's own values were eyeballed. Say if you would rather have
the mock-up's numbers back — it is a small change either way.
