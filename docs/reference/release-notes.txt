0.4.0 — the liquid-glass material, and the corner controls.

Check the version first. Long-press the app icon, App info. It should read
0.4.0. Every build from here carries a new number, so if it says something
else the install did not take and nothing below applies.

The corner controls, top left and top right. The icon was being drawn in the
top-left corner of its own circle instead of the middle of it — a round button
that never centred what was in it. They also went from a black disc with a lit
rim to actual glass: they were carrying a 94% blackout meant for the nav bar,
which floats over text and must not be read through. Nothing sits behind a
corner control but the background, so it was paying for a risk it does not
have.

The glass. Four changes, in the order they matter:

  - The edge is a complete bright hairline now, all the way round. It used to
    fade to nothing across about three quarters of the perimeter, so the pane
    had no visible boundary along its bottom and right and read as a smudge
    rather than as an object.
  - The light comes from the top centre, not off the top-left corner. On a bar
    of three equal tabs a corner light lands as a bright patch over the first
    tab with the other two in shade — it looks like a stain on the glass.
  - The sheen is two bands crossing the pane rather than one fade pouring off
    a corner. A fade says "lit from over there"; a band says "this is a curved
    surface catching one light".
  - The shadow is one wide soft fall instead of two or three stacked dark
    ones. Stacked shadows under a pane that exists to pass light put a bruise
    on the ground under it.

Still this app's own numbers, not the library's: the refraction strength. It
is close — the same chromatic aberration, and a bend that reaches the same
maximum — but it scales with the size of the component where the library uses
one fixed figure.

What to look at: the nav bar over a screen that scrolls under it, and the two
controls in the top corners.
