0.9.0 — the theme wipe, and one less control on the cabinet tab.

Check the version first: long-press the app icon, App info. It must read 0.9.0.

The app opens in LIGHT now, whatever the phone is set to. The design is drawn
light first, and light is the scheme that reads standing outdoors at a cabinet
in daylight, which is where this app gets used.

The theme wipe covers the whole screen now, and it runs the right way.

It was covering only a thin frame. The wipe is a curtain of the outgoing
colour, drawn as part of the ground. The ground is painted twice — once across
the window, once inside the shell so the glass has something to sample — and
only the outer copy was drawing the curtain. The inner copy carries the NEW
colour and sits on top, so it painted the curtain out everywhere except the
17dp margin. The page inside snapped instantly while a border wiped around it.
That is the override over the top bar. The curtain now belongs to the ground
itself, so every copy carries it and none can cover another's.

Direction now depends on which way you are going. Turning dark on, the old
light page is held at the top and the dark one rises from the floor. Turning
it off, the old dark page is held at the bottom and the light one comes down
from above. Dark rises off the ground, light falls from the sky. One direction
for both made one of the two feel backwards.

Watch a tab change. Material's fade-through: the old screen fades out over
90ms, the new one fades in and grows from 92% over 210ms after it. Tabs are
siblings so nothing slides — neither is after the other. An instant cut
between two pages of similar weight is the cheapest-feeling thing an app can
do, and a tab change is the one moment the user triggers on purpose and
watches.

The nav bar sits 8dp lower, into the gutter rather than mid-air.

The cabinet tab has one control fewer. The strip naming your boxes — 04, 07 —
is gone. It did nothing tapping a door on the render does not already do:
the two handlers were the same two lines. It was a second, smaller, abstract
copy of the cabinet, sitting directly beneath a picture of the cabinet, and
the picture is the better control — the doors are lit, numbered, and where the
receiver is already looking. What is left says which box the button opens and
when it must be collected, which is all the strip was really for.

The render now sits in a recess. It is a thing you look into, and that is what
a recess is for. It is also where the tab's depth went when the strip was
removed, since the strip was the only recessed thing on it.

Known, unchanged: the Dark mode switch on Settings is a speckled blob in dark
mode only — in light it draws its sun correctly. BUG-003.

One rough edge worth naming. During the 480ms wipe, text in the part not yet
revealed carries the new scheme's colour on the old scheme's ground, so for a
moment it is pale on pale. That is the cost of the curtain being BEHIND the
content, which is deliberate: the alternative covers everything and transitions
nothing.
