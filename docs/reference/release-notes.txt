0.8.2 — the square over the top bar, and a calmer bend in the dark.

Check the version first: long-press the app icon, App info. It must read 0.8.2.

The square is gone.

It was the page's own light, painted twice. Every screen stands on a ground
with an accent light falling from the top. That ground is painted once across
the whole window, and once more inside the shell, because the glass can only
bend what has been recorded and the recording starts inside the gutter.

Two copies is fine. Two copies that disagree about where the top of the page
is, is not. Each started its light at its own first row, and the inner one
starts about 78 pixels lower — so inside the gutter the page carried two
lights instead of one and came out warmer than the margin around it. That is
the whole of it: a rectangle from x 34 to 746 and y 78 to 166, warmer by four
or five counts out of 255. It showed at the top and nowhere else because the
light has run out by 900 pixels, and it took the accent colour, so it read
orange in light and blue in dark.

The inner copy is now told how far down it sits, and its light picks up
exactly where the one underneath had got to. Measured across the gutter edge:
the step was 6 counts, it is now 1. At the top edge it was 4, now 1.

This was not new in 0.8.1. The same rectangle measures identically in 0.8.0 —
it has been there since the ground was first painted twice, which was the fix
that made the nav bar stop being see-through.

Dark mode bends less now. Half as much, and light mode is untouched.

The bend is the same geometry in both, so this is not a correction, it is the
material behaving differently on the two pages. A lens moves pixels, and
moving a pixel only shows where its neighbour differs. The dark page is
near-black ground under near-white type, the highest contrast anywhere in the
app, so the same displacement drags bright letters into the rim and reads as a
smear. The light page is dark type on pale steel, a much shorter step, and the
same bend reads as glass.

Half is the library's own figure — it halves its displacement for the case it
finds difficult too.
