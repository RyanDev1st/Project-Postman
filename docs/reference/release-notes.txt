0.8.1 — the top bar, and a real edge on the glass.

Check the version first: long-press the app icon, App info. It must read 0.8.1.

Look at the three round buttons in the top bar — menu, scan, bell.

Until this build they were not glass at all. They were being handed no
backdrop, so they fell through to the plain fallback: a flat disc of ground
colour at 30% with a ring drawn round it. A material nothing else on the
screen uses, sitting in the corner of every screen. That is the fault over the
top bar.

They were given none because the bar sat inside the very recording the glass
reads, and a pane cannot sample a layer it is part of. The bar is now drawn
over the content instead of above it in the flow, the same way the bottom nav
already was, so the beads can read what is behind them. It is also the best
place in the app for glass to be: the light on the page runs out well before
the foot of the screen, so the top is the one region with anything behind the
glass worth bending.

The rim is no longer notched. Every pane was being clipped a second time with
a path clip, and a path clip on a phone's canvas has hard edges — no
smoothing. Along the nav bar's long sides the steps were too small to notice.
Around a 30dp circle the whole rim was visibly stepped.

The glass now has a real edge. The library ships three displacement images and
this port used the default, which measured out as a flat ramp corner to corner
— a uniform shrink of the backdrop, no thickness. The one called "prominent"
is shaped instead: nothing at all through the middle, then all of the work in
two bands near the rim. That is a bevel, and it is what a thick piece of glass
does to what is behind it. A flat ramp cannot make one at any strength.

Its blue channel is empty, and the library reads y from blue for every mode,
so its own "prominent" setting is a fixed shove upwards rather than a
displacement. This reads green, which is the channel with the picture in it.

The shadow is the library's now: twelve pixels down, forty of blur, a quarter
black. That is a wide shallow pool, not an elevation, and `Modifier.shadow`
cannot make one — it asks the platform what a solid object at that height
would cast, which is much tighter and much darker at the edge.

Honest limit, unchanged and worth saying again. This material shows what is
behind it. The ground under the bottom nav is one flat near-black, so there is
very little for it to show. The top bar now looks better than the bottom one
for exactly that reason, and no further work on the glass will change it —
what would change it is putting some light on the lower half of the page.

Still open: the Dark mode switch on Settings is a speckled grey blob, BUG-003.
Not caused by this.
