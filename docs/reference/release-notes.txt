Design rebuild — first build checked against the mock-up before sending.

What changed, and why it should look different:

1. The glass. Blur drops from 24px to 6px and saturation from 190% to 140%,
   which are the liquid-glass defaults Ryan asked for. The old numbers blurred
   four times too hard, so there was nothing left behind the pane to refract.
   Also gone: a near-black layer painted under the white film that was never in
   the design at all, and was turning frosted glass into a tinted window.

2. The dark-mode toggle. The faint rings round the knob now stop at the edge of
   the pill, as they should — clipped they read as sky lightening round the
   sun; loose they were circles floating on the screen. The knob well was also
   too bright, and the pill had no shadow of its own.

3. The nav bar. Every icon and label was pinned to the left edge of its own
   tab. They sit in the middle now. Labels are the right size and case, and the
   selected pill has its lit top edge back, so it reads as a raised pane rather
   than a flat coloured lozenge.

4. The cabinet camera. Tapping to go wide used to jerk the picture sideways
   before pulling back, and left the next push-in starting from the wrong
   place. Scale and pan now travel together, 720ms each way.

5. The map. It frames itself to the walk instead of a zoom worked out for a
   card of an assumed size. The walk shown starts at the academic block — it is
   a fixed route to the gate, not one from where you are standing.

6. The card. Its shadow is tucked under the foot rather than thrown evenly on
   all sides, and its fill no longer breaks when the theme changes.

What to look at first: the nav bar over a scrolling screen, and the dark-mode
toggle in Settings.

Not done yet: the full liquid-glass material — its edge gradients, its
displacement scale and its chromatic aberration are still this app's own
numbers, not the library's. This build changes the blur and the saturation
only.
