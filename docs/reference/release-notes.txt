The redesign, ported into the app from the mock-up.

Three tabs — Home, Cabinet, Settings — with the sign-in flow in front of
them. Home is a claim ticket: the door number, the time left draining along
the foot, the map card that answers *where to walk*, and the ledger of what
you collected. The Cabinet tab shows the real render with every door that is
yours lit in amber — the tint keeps the stencilled number, because the number
is what you carry. The panel under it is the same object in every state.

The design's own system, ported token by token from the mock-up's CSS: dark
leads, light is a second design with the accent going burnt orange, and the
amber door light is identical in both schemes. Three materials — glass on the
nav, cards lifted off the ground, recesses set into it.

Nothing talks to a server yet, so every parcel on screen is made up. The
design sources now live in docs/designs/mockup/, next to the port.

Fixed since the last build, after a second round of screenshots:

  The Dark mode row has its real component now — the sun-and-moon switch
  from the design, not a plain toggle. Clouds fall out, stars come down,
  and the moon slides across the sun.
  The theme change is one pass again. It used to cover the screen with a
  blank rectangle for half a second; the curtain now runs behind the
  content, so every card and letter stays put and recolours in place.
  The glass is glass. There was no blur behind it at all, so the nav was
  a window and the word COLLECTED could be read straight through it.
  Every screen has its light back. The accent wash was being painted over
  by the opaque ground on top of it.
  The Cabinet tab moves. Tapping a door worked out where to push in to and
  then never went, and the automatic push-in for a single box did the same.

Also new: the map card is a real map — Google's tiles in the app's own two
schemes, the walk drawn over them, and your own position on it. It needs a
key, and this build has none, so the card still shows the drawn plan. That
one is not testable yet.

What to look at: the theme switch in Settings, both directions — the
component itself and the change behind it. Then hold the nav bar over the
cabinet render and check you cannot read anything through it. Then the
Cabinet tab: tap a door and see whether the camera lands on it.
