0.8.0 — the glass is a lens now, not a painted panel.

Check the version first: long-press the app icon, App info. It must read 0.8.0.

Scroll Home so the COLLECTED list runs under the nav bar. Nothing behind the
bar should be readable — not in the middle, and not at the two ends. On 0.7.0
the middle was soft but the words at each end stayed sharp, and there was a
hard vertical seam near the right-hand end. Both are gone.

What was wrong, and it was not a matter of taste:

1. The white gradients were on the wrong part of the component. The library
   Ryan asked for puts them in a ring a pixel and a half wide, masked out of
   the middle. This app painted them across the whole face. White across the
   face is the definition of frost, and no amount of lowering the numbers was
   going to fix where they were. They are now the rim, and the face carries
   nothing but a flat veil.

2. The lens was squashing the picture. The library's displacement image is
   covered over the pane, keeping its shape. This app stretched it to fit, and
   on a bar six times wider than it is tall that pulls six times harder
   downwards than sideways — the horizontal smear across the bar.

3. The lens was reaching outside the recorded screen and finding nothing.
   Where it found nothing the bar was simply transparent, so the page showed
   through at full sharpness. That is the sharp words at the two ends.

4. The blur was running before the lens, and a blur quietly grows the frame it
   hands on. Measured on the emulator, the lens was working in a frame 887
   pixels wide while it had been told 810, so it was aiming 37 pixels off
   centre — an error on one side only, which is what the seam was.

None of the four is a setting. They were found by measuring, not by taste:
the library's image was decoded and read pixel by pixel, and the app was made
to paint its own coordinates on screen so they could be read back.

One honest addition that is NOT in the library. Over the ground at the foot of
this app's screens there is one flat near-black and nothing else, and blurring
a flat colour returns the same flat colour. Ported exactly, the bar came out
as a ring around nothing. So the glass now lifts very slightly over dark
content, the way Apple's own dark material does. Flat, about seven percent.

Also in this build: a control shrinks by four percent while held, and its
highlight only appears under a finger. Both are the library's.

Known and not fixed here: the Dark mode switch on Settings is a speckled grey
blob. It is logged as BUG-003. It is not caused by this change — the two plain
toggles above it on the same screen are clean.
