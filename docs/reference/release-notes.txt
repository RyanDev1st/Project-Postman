0.7.0 — the blur works. Seen working, not hoped for.

Check the version: long-press the app icon, App info. It must read 0.7.0.

Scroll Home so the COLLECTED list passes under the nav bar. Those words should
be soft, dissolved, unreadable. Until this build they were razor sharp, which
is what Ryan spotted from a screenshot and called a transparent knockoff. He
was right, and it was not a matter of degree — there was no blur at all.

Three faults stacked on top of each other, each hiding the next:

1. The blur was attached to the layer that records the screen. That layer is
   never drawn to a canvas, and an effect only happens when its layer is
   drawn. The layer that does reach the screen took its effect from the
   refraction shader alone, and that shader needs Android 13 — below it the
   value is nothing, so the line cleared the effect instead of setting one.

2. With that fixed the bar was still see-through, because what the glass
   samples is a recording of the page, and the page's background was painted
   by a parent outside that recording. The glass was sampling a transparent
   sheet. Blur a transparent sheet and you get a transparent sheet.

3. Moving the background in did nothing at first, because "inside" is a matter
   of modifier order, and it was still declared on the wrong side of the
   recording.

None of these are settings. No amount of tuning alphas, gradients or light
direction — five builds of it — could have reached them.

**Everything else in this build is unchanged.** The material's numbers are the
same as 0.6.0. What changed is that they now apply to something.

One honest limit. This material is a lens: it shows what is behind it,
blurred and saturated, and has little substance of its own. Over the parcel
cards and the cabinet render it should now read as glass. Over empty
background it will always be quiet, because our ground is near-black and flat
and there is nothing there to bend.
